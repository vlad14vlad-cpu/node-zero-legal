NODE ZERO — Privacy Policy

Updated: 7 October 2026 · Android

1. Publisher and contact

This notice is provided by the developer of NODE ZERO. Privacy contact: vlad14vlad@gmail.com. Published online policy. Accepting the Terms of Service does not give consent to personalized advertising.

2. Where information is stored

The developer does not operate a player-account, payment or cloud-save backend and does not maintain a personal-data database on developer-run servers. Accounts, purchases and cloud saves are handled by Google Play services. If you make your player profile public (see Public player profile), that profile is stored in Google Firebase on the developer's behalf.

The game stores progress, settings, unlocks, purchase-delivery records and saved runs on your device. The Android widget stores a local progress summary. Diagnostic errors are kept locally and can be copied by you for support; these diagnostic logs are not automatically uploaded. Purchase records help prevent duplicate delivery and do not contain card or bank details.

3. Google Play Games

If Google Play Games signs you in, the app can access your Play Games player identifier, display name and profile image, synchronize progress through Saved Games, and submit leaderboard scores. Cloud synchronization may occur automatically while signed in. Your score and profile may be publicly visible according to Google's settings. Google hosts these services; the developer does not receive your Google password.

4. Purchases

Google Play processes payments. The app receives product identifiers, purchase status and transaction identifiers/tokens to grant and restore purchases and prevent duplicate delivery. The developer does not receive your payment-card details. Payment records and refunds are managed by Google Play under its policies and applicable law.

5. Rewarded advertising

Google AdMob and its advertising services may process IP addresses, device/advertising identifiers, app interactions and diagnostic data for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Google may use an IP address to infer approximate location. The app does not request precise device location for advertising. Ads may preload once Google's consent system permits requests; watching an ad is optional.

Where required, Google's consent form offers privacy choices before ad requests. You can revisit available choices through AD PRIVACY OPTIONS in Settings. Declining personalized ads does not prevent ordinary gameplay; ad availability depends on your choices and Google's requirements. Acceptance of the game terms is not advertising consent.

Public player profile (optional)

The game has a player profile (dossier). It is private unless you choose MAKE PUBLIC. When public, the game signs your Google Play Games account into Google Firebase Authentication and stores one profile document in Google Cloud Firestore, keyed by your Play Games player identifier. The document contains your Play Games display name, chosen profile theme and title, earned badges, equipped core and core collection, Pilot levels, game records (such as best waves, kills, bosses, runs, rebirths and campaign progress), up to three showcased achievements, whether VIP is active and whether you own the Founder edition, plus the time it was last updated. Other players can open it, for example from the leaderboards. It never contains your save file, Scrap balance, purchase history, payment details, email address or profile image (other players see the image Google Play Games already shows on leaderboards).

The profile is used only to show it to other players; it is not used for advertising. Switching the profile back to private (CUSTOMIZE → PUBLIC DOSSIER) deletes the profile document and the Firebase account created for it. Private profiles are never uploaded. You can also request deletion by emailing the privacy contact above with your Play Games display name.

Optional game analytics

If you choose ALLOW ANALYTICS, the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to measure app usage and gameplay, including tutorial progress, runs, wave milestones and completed rewarded ads. Google may process an app installation identifier, app/device information, IP-derived approximate location and usage events. The game does not request precise location or send your Play Games identity, cloud-save contents or debug logs to Analytics. Analytics advertising-ID collection and advertising personalization are disabled; advertising uses its separate privacy choices.

Analytics is off until you choose to allow it. Declining does not prevent gameplay. Change your choice in Settings → Privacy & Terms → GAME ANALYTICS. Turning it off stops collection and resets the Analytics data stored on this device; it does not erase data already received by Google. Contact the developer about Analytics data requests. Google processes data under its published privacy policy.

6. Other connections and support

The in-game mailbox retrieves public news from GitHub-hosted content. The hosting service receives connection information such as your IP address. External links are handled by their destination services. Notifications, audio preferences and widget state are managed on your device. If you choose to email support, the developer receives the address and information you send and uses it to respond to your request.

7. Retention and control

Local data remains until it is cleared or the app is removed, subject to Android backup behavior. Clearing local data does not delete cloud saves, Google purchase history or leaderboard data; cloud progress can return when you sign in again. Use Google Play Games settings to manage or delete game data, and Google's account controls for data Google holds. Deleting a game save does not cancel payment records or refund purchases. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations.

8. Privacy rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability and withdrawal of consent, and the right to complain to a supervisory authority. Withdrawal does not affect earlier lawful processing. Contact the developer for information actually held in support correspondence; use Google's privacy controls for its accounts and services. Service providers may process information internationally under their published safeguards.

9. Children and updates

NODE ZERO is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. A parent or guardian should contact support if a child has provided information to the developer. This notice may be updated when the app's practices change.
