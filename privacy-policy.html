Privacy Policy — NODE ZERO

Last updated: August 11, 2026

This policy explains what data NODE ZERO ("the App", "the Game") collects, how it's used, and your choices — written specifically for what this app actually does, not a generic template.

1. Who this applies to

NODE ZERO is not directed at children. It is intended for a general audience and is not designed, marketed, or targeted specifically at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

2. What data the App itself collects

None, directly. NODE ZERO has no account system, no sign-in, no server backend, and does not collect your name, email, or any personal identifiers itself. Your progress — gold, scrap, VIP status, upgrade levels, and settings — is stored only on your own device, using Android/iOS's standard local app storage. We never see this data; it never leaves your device unless one of the third-party services below is involved.

If you uninstall the App, this local data is deleted with it. There is no server-side copy for us to delete on request, because none exists.

3. Third-party services used by the App

The App integrates two third-party services, each of which has its own separate data practices we don't control:

ServiceWhat it's used forWhat it may collect
Google AdMob Shows optional rewarded video ads (you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game reward — ads are never shown without you tapping to request one). Device/advertising identifiers, IP address, general location (country/region), app usage data, and — only if you consent — data used for ad personalization. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses data from AdMob.
Google Play Billing (Android) / App Store payments (iOS) Processes the optional VIP purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Google/Apple — we never see or store your payment details. See Google's Privacy Policy or Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. Your consent choices (EU / UK / California)

If you're in the EU, UK, or a US state with applicable privacy law, the App shows a consent message (via Google's User Messaging Platform) before any ad-related data is collected, letting you choose whether to allow personalized ads, non-personalized ads only, or — depending on your region's requirements — decline entirely. You can change this choice at any time from the in-game pause menu via Privacy Options (only shown where applicable to your account/region).

5. Data sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not share any data with third parties ourselves, beyond the AdMob and payment processing described above, which are necessary for the App's ad-supported and purchase functionality to work at all.

6. Your rights

Since we hold no personal data on our own servers, there is nothing for us to export or delete on request beyond what's already described above. For data held by Google (AdMob, Play Billing) or Apple, use their respective account privacy controls or the links in Section 3.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
